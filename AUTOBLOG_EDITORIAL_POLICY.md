# Auto-blog editorial and SEO policy

## Purpose

The auto-blog may create useful, source-based drafts about Dholera SIR. It must not publish search-engine-first pages, manufacture facts, or replace editorial review. Increased reach should come from accurate coverage, clear topic focus, useful internal links, and consistent quality.

## Mandatory rules

1. Every generated item remains `published: false` and `isApproved: false` until an administrator reviews it. WordPress automation must also use `draft` status.
2. The supplied news source must be named and linked near the beginning. The draft must not invent sources, quotations, approvals, dates, prices, statistics, distances, or project status.
3. The article must add original explanatory value and must not reproduce long passages from the source.
4. Claims of guaranteed returns, assured profit, risk-free investment, false urgency, and similar promotional language are prohibited.
5. A web article must link naturally to at least two relevant evergreen pages selected from the approved Dholera Platform routes. Placeholder and legacy `/blog/` links are rejected.
6. Generated article bodies must not contain an H1, scripts, iframes, objects, embeds, fake schema, phone placeholders, or unsupported markup.
7. Titles and recent topics are compared for similarity. A rewritten headline about an existing event must be rejected or merged with the existing article instead of creating a competing URL.
8. SEO titles must be 35-60 characters and descriptions 120-160 characters. They must accurately summarize the article rather than promise rankings or repeat keyword variants.
9. Images must be relevant to the story. Administrators must confirm image ownership, accuracy, ALT text, and whether an image is illustrative before publishing.
10. Material claims about government policy, infrastructure, land, approvals, or timelines must be checked against a primary source before approval. If primary confirmation is unavailable, the article must clearly attribute the claim and describe the uncertainty.

## Human approval checklist

- Open and verify the source link.
- Check the headline, dates, names, figures, project status, and attribution.
- Confirm the draft provides information beyond a paraphrased headline.
- Remove speculation, repeated keywords, irrelevant sections, and aggressive sales language.
- Check that internal links are relevant and resolve on the `www` domain.
- Add descriptive image ALT text and confirm image rights.
- Confirm the canonical slug is descriptive and does not compete with an existing page.
- Publish only after all checks pass; otherwise revise or reject the draft.

## Measurement

Evaluate the system by indexed useful pages, impressions for relevant queries, engaged visits, qualified enquiries, corrections, and rejected-draft rate. Do not evaluate it by publishing volume alone.
